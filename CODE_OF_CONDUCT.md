# CakeSplitter Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CakeSplitter contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate with
respect, assume good faith, give technically specific feedback, and make space
for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private
information, and pressuring anyone to disclose private files or security
evidence.

## Project boundaries

Keep issue reports, pull requests, examples, and test fixtures free of real
private files, credentials, tokens, unredacted local paths, and raw
vulnerability evidence. Report suspected vulnerabilities through the private
process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not through public project channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict
participation when conduct threatens people, project safety, or the local-only
privacy boundary. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented
privately where appropriate, and applied consistently.

For sensitive conduct involving GitHub-hosted content, use GitHub's private
reporting and abuse-reporting controls. Non-sensitive project conduct concerns
may be raised in a focused issue without including private personal evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in CakeSplitter project spaces and when representing the
project in public spaces.
